Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she saw a big bird with a pretty wing. Lily felt very alert. She wanted to help the bird.
Lily said, "Hi bird! Can you help me? I need to heal my wing." The bird looked at her and said, "I can't fly. My wing is hurt." Lily wanted to help the bird, so she took it to her mom.
Lily's mom told her, "Remember, the bird is a good thing to do. It cannot hurt you." Lily listened to her mom and followed her home. She was happy that she could help the bird. The bird became Lily's friend, and they played in the garden every day.
